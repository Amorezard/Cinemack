# Round 37 (12 Nov 2026): genre rule refined, TV in onboarding, Trakt export upload, form alignment (website only)

On top of round 36 (commit 355a0ec, installed 09:28; GENRE_MAIN=1 live).

## 1. Genre rule (GENRE_MAIN)
Round 36's report on the real data (5,025 films with 1,000+ votes, 5,023 with IMDb genres) showed the plain agreement
rule (TMDB genre kept only if IMDb also lists it) removes genres IMDb's three slots have no room for: Knives Out left
Mystery, Star Wars / Avatar / WALL-E left Science Fiction, Se7en / The Silence of the Lambs / The Shining left Thriller,
Inception / The Dark Knight left Action. Thriller went 1,454 -> 746, Science Fiction 777 -> 462.
web/genres.py now has variants (GENRE_MAIN=1|2|3):
- 1 every TMDB genre needs IMDb's agreement (round 36)
- 2 only broad genres need it (Drama, Comedy, Action, Adventure, Family, Fantasy, Thriller, Romance); specific ones
  (Science Fiction, Horror, Mystery, Crime, War, Western, History, Music, Documentary, Animation) stay as TMDB lists them
- 3 as 2, and TMDB's first-listed genre always stays
tools/genre_gold.py scores 0 (TMDB), 1, 2, 3 on 46 hand-labelled films (Claude's labels, NOT blind: written after
round 36's report). Rule written before the run: highest score wins; a tie keeps 1; otherwise the lower number.
todo.sh writes the winner to site.env. Result: results/genre_gold.txt.

## 2. Form alignment
"Age rating limit" and "Longest film" (onboarding step 3) were not level: the field without a hint was stretched to
the row height and its rows spread out. `.field { align-content: start }` fixes every form (Chromium: 46/59 -> 46/46 px).

## 3. Trakt
"Connect Trakt" is OAuth and needs TRAKT_CLIENT_ID (a Trakt API app) in keys.env; it was never set. Trakt's own export
is free (trakt.tv Settings > Data > Export: a ZIP of JSON files in the API's shapes), so the history step and
Settings > Imports now take those .json files (several at once): trakt_sync.import_export, kind "trakt" on
/api/v8/import. History items keep Trakt's ids as keys, the same as the OAuth sync, so nothing is counted twice.
To switch Connect Trakt on later: app at trakt.tv/oauth/applications, redirect
REDACTED/trakt/callback, TRAKT_CLIENT_ID in keys.env, restart.

## 4. TV in onboarding (web/tv_prefs.py)
"And TV?" in the taste step: 12 kinds of shows (Love it / Not for me; hand-picked posters, query fills gaps) and 10
well-known shows (Watching = follow / Loved it / Not for me / Haven't seen). users.db tables tv_prefs, tv_groups.
Routes GET /api/v8/onboarding/tv, POST /api/v8/onboarding/tv/groups, POST /api/v8/tv/choice.
Pick Tonight shows: marked shows are left out; +0.25 for a loved kind, +0.1 per genre shared with loved shows (max 2),
-0.4 for a "not for me" kind (the score is a weighted rating, about 7-9).
Not yet: TV and film taste editable in Profile > Settings; chat show recommendations do not read tv_prefs.
