# Round 39 (12 Nov 2026): the MovieLens tag genome as a third genre vote (GENRE_MAIN variant 4)

Round 37's variant 2 (live) keeps specific genres from TMDB and needs IMDb's agreement only for broad ones (Drama,
Comedy, Action, Adventure, Family, Fantasy, Thriller, Romance). IMDb lists at most three genres, so some broad genres
were still lost: Inception and The Dark Knight (Action), Se7en and The Silence of the Lambs (Thriller), Zootopia
(Comedy), La La Land (Romance). Variant 4 adds the MovieLens tag genome (already in data/genome/genome.npz since 23 Oct;
relevance 0..1 of ~1,100 tags for ~13,800 films, from what people tagged and rated):
- tools/build_genre_votes.py -> logs/genre_votes.db: per film, the highest relevance among each broad genre's tag
  names found in the genome; per genre, a cut-off = the 25th percentile among films where TMDB and IMDb both list the
  genre (set by the data, not by the labelled films).
- A broad genre IMDb did not list stays when the genome scores it at or above the cut-off. Films outside the genome
  (mostly after 2019) behave as variant 2.
- tools/genre_gold.py scores 0-4 on the same 46 labelled films (not blind); rule written before the run: highest
  score wins, a tie keeps the live variant (2), else the lower number. The winner goes into site.env.
Tests: tests/test_r39_genres.py (3) with a fake genome.
