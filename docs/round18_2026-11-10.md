# Round 18 (Tue 10 Nov 2026): shorts, TV rows on Home, calendar week and month

The requests came from screenshots of the live site after round 17. Nothing in the answer pipeline, retrieval,
evaluation or `facts.db` changed.

## 1. "This isn't a movie" (a 7-minute short, film page said "0 h 7 min")
- Rule: a film with a known runtime of **40 minutes or less** is a short (the Academy's definition). It is left out of
  every film row on Home (hero, In Theaters, Recommended, Top Rated, Trending, Opening Soon, Back on the Big Screen)
  and the Release Calendar. Unknown runtime is kept: many films have no runtime until they open.
- Still shown: search, the chat, the person's own saved films, and the film page, which now says "7 min · Short film".
- Runtime format everywhere (`core.rt`): `7 min`, `2h`, `1h 58m` (was `0h 07m` on cards and `0 h 7 min` on the film page).
- Code: `discovery.no_shorts`, `SHORT_MAX = 40`; Top Rated and Trending exclude shorts in their SQL.

## 2. TV on Home: New Seasons and Top Rated Shows
`facts.db` has no TV ratings or TV genres, so these rows need data from TMDB. `tools/fetch_home_extra.py` fetches it into
a separate file, `logs/tmdb_extra.db`, read only by the website (`web/tmdb_extra.py`). `facts.db` is opened read-only.
A run writes a temporary file and swaps it in only if at least 40 top-rated shows came back.

| Row | Eligible | Ranking (ties: TMDB id) |
|---|---|---|
| New Seasons | a season (season 1 = new series, labelled "New series") that premiered in the last 30 days, from `facts.db` seasons plus the fetched ones | TMDB popularity, then newest premiere |
| Top Rated Shows | TMDB's top-rated TV list (10 pages, 200 shows), 300+ votes | weighted rating WR = v/(v+1000)·R + 1000/(v+1000)·C, C = mean rating of the other fetched shows with 300+ votes (7.0 if fewer than 10) |

- News, talk, soap and reality shows are left out of both rows.
- TMDB TV genres are mapped onto the 18 film genres (Action & Adventure → Action, Adventure; Sci-Fi & Fantasy →
  Science Fiction, Fantasy; War & Politics → War; Kids → Family), so the Home genre chips filter the TV rows with the
  same rules as the films. A show with no genre data matches no genre filter. Every genre = no filter.
- Cards: poster opens the show sheet (Follow, alerts, Track progress), the bell follows in place, and the meta line shows
  the TMDB rating and "Season 3 · Nov 2" (New Seasons) or the network (Top Rated Shows).
- A show followed from Home that `facts.db` doesn't know still appears on the Watchlist and in alerts: `tv_following`,
  `v8extra._shows` and `_seasons` fall back to the fetched data. The calendar also lists fetched season premieres.
- If the file is missing (no TMDB key, fetch failed), Top Rated Shows is hidden and New Seasons uses `facts.db` only.
- The fetcher also fills posters, backdrops and runtimes for list and calendar films that `facts.db` lacks: round 17
  found 54 of 81 upcoming films without a poster. It makes at most 300 film requests.
- Freshness: round 18 runs the fetcher once, and `nightly.sh` gets one line so it refreshes nightly. The nightly script
  already skips nights when an evaluation is running.

## 3. Calendar Week view: every release, nothing cut off
- Old behaviour: fixed 330 px columns, two 2:3 posters per group, "+N more", and the posters were cropped at the
  column's bottom edge.
- Now each column lists every release of its day, in group cards (In Theaters, Streaming, TV) with icons, like the
  approved reference.
  - A quiet group (1–2 films, on a day with at most 6 releases) gets wide backdrop tiles with the title over them.
  - Busier groups and days get compact rows: a 32 px poster, the title, and genre · runtime or the streaming service.
  - TV is always shown as rows ("Season 4").
- Columns in a week share the busiest day's height, so a busy week is taller rather than clipped.
- A film links to its film page and a show opens its show sheet. The day header selects the day and opens the
  day panel, which keeps the actions.
- Accessibility: the day headers are toggle buttons in a labelled group; the phone day strip remains a tablist.

## 4. Calendar Month view: bigger and legible
- Cells are 128 px tall (140 px at 1440 px and wider; 104 px on tablets; 72 px on phones). Day numbers are 17 px,
  the count is a badge, the dots are larger, and two posters of 42×63 px show from tablet width up (none on phones,
  where the panel below lists the day).
- The selected day has a yellow frame and number; today is a yellow circle.
- The day panel (beside the grid from 1280 px, otherwise below) follows the reference: one card per group with
  compact rows (poster, title, genre · runtime, the service or "not announced yet"), plus icon actions: Showtimes,
  Where to watch, Watchlist and Follow.
- **Add reminders**: saves every film of that day that isn't saved yet and follows every show not followed yet. It
  only appears on today or later and only when something is left to add. Saved films and followed shows produce
  alerts under the bell, as before.
