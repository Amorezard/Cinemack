## Website

**TV**
- **Show page `#/show/<id>`** (new; `web/tvdata.py`, `web/v8/show.js`):
  - facts, IMDb and TMDB ratings with vote counts, and the US TV rating;
  - where to watch, the next episode with its date, the creators and the trailer (opens YouTube);
  - every season with its average IMDb rating;
  - each episode: date, title, synopsis, still, runtime, IMDb and TMDB rating, and finale or mid-season finale;
  - "Watched up to here" (records progress), the highest-rated episodes on IMDb (300+ votes), and the cast.
  - All TV links across the site now go here: Home rows, search, Calendar, Continue Watching, Pick Tonight.
- **Episodes**: TMDB episode lists (`tv_episodes`, round 19) are joined to IMDb episode ratings by the show's IMDb id
  and season and episode numbers. Nothing is copied between tables.
- **Continue Watching**:
  - the real next episode, with its title and date ("S3 E5 airs Nov 12");
  - imported Netflix episodes, which have a season and title but no number, are matched to TMDB's episode list by title.
- **Calendar**:
  - every episode of the shows the person follows ("S2 E4");
  - season finales of shows with 200+ TMDB votes;
  - season premieres as before. Episode 1 isn't listed twice.
- **Top Rated Shows** is ranked by IMDb: shows with 20,000+ IMDb votes, weighted
  v/(v+5000)·R + 5000/(v+5000)·7.2, with news, talk, soap and reality shows left out. TMDB's list is the fallback.
  This replaces round 18's TMDB-only ranking, which put a 9.4 from few votes near the top.
- **Pick Tonight: Movie or show?** (new first choice). TV picks are ranked in code from facts.db, with no model call:
  - **who's watching** maps to US TV ratings (kids → TV-PG or milder);
  - **time**: episodes must be no longer than the time chosen;
  - **mood** maps to TV genres; Scary uses the horror keyword;
  - **services** and never-genres are hard limits; a show whose fact is unknown is left out;
  - shows already followed or watched are left out;
  - **ranking**: "familiar" = weighted IMDb rating plus a little weight on popularity; "different" = under 50,000
    IMDb votes.
  - Each pick lists factual reasons (genre, episode length, rating and votes, seasons, services). "Try different
    shows" and loosening a limit work as for films.

**Accounts**
- **Header**: when signed out, the header shows Sign in and Create account. Below 1280 px only Sign in shows; Create
  account stays in the avatar menu.
- **Home**: a "Make Cinemack yours" card (Create account, Sign in, Not now) for signed-out visitors, and "Finish
  setting up" for accounts that skipped set-up.
- **Create account `#/join`** (new; `web/v8/join.js`), five steps; only the first is required:
  1. **Account**: username, password twice, display name, the HTTPS warning, and moving guest data into the account.
  2. **Taste**: kinds of film to love or avoid, and well-known films to mark liked / not for me / not seen.
  3. **What you watch**: services, never-recommend genres, an age-rating limit, the longest film, decades, languages.
  4. **History**: Netflix or Letterboxd file, or Trakt.
  5. **Profile**: display name, privacy for five things (private by default), appearance.

  Finish saves through the existing `/api/onboarding` and `/api/v8/privacy` routes. Every "Create account" link on
  the site now opens this flow.

**Private chat** (the classic page had it): a lock button next to New chat (and in the phone drawer). It sends
`X-MB-Private: 1`, which the server already honoured:
- the chat isn't saved and nothing is remembered;
- the person's history and taste aren't used;
- a violet banner explains this, with "End private chat".

## 3b long-chat summaries (switch `LONG_SUMMARY=1`, default 0 = the old behaviour)
- **Problem**:
  - the answer model sees the last 12 messages cut to 6,000 characters;
  - the planner sees 4 turns verbatim;
  - the page itself sent only the last 12 messages.

  A limit stated in turn 1 was gone by turn 7.
- **Fix** (`web/longsum.py`, no model call):
  - a short "Earlier in this conversation" note at the top of the answer prompt: the person's own words for each cut
    turn, and the first sentence of each cut answer;
  - one line of the limits the person stated anywhere in the chat, from the 3c detector;
  - turn 1 is always kept; the note is at most 1,600 characters;
  - the planner gets the same limits line and the earlier questions (`conv.py`);
  - the page now sends up to 40 messages; with the switch off, the server still uses only the last 12.
- **Tuning rule** (written ~14:00, before any LONG_SUMMARY run; in `tools/run_3b_tune.sh`):
  - on `sets/tune_v8_long.json`: ON ≥ 5/6 and ON ≥ OFF;
  - on `sets/multiturn.json` retrieval, ON loses at most 1 conversation.

  `tune_v8_long.json` (written today; not blind) has 6 twelve-turn chats with 12,300+ characters of history; turn 1
  sets the limit and the last turn asks for a film.
  - If the rule passes, LONG_SUMMARY=1 is proposed for `v8_lock.env` and the user decides at the freeze.
- **Scoring** (`tools/long_eval.py`, no judge): the final answer must name at least one of its source films, and
  every named film must meet the limit, checked in facts.db; an unknown fact fails.
  - It runs through a copy of the website server (port 49160, answer cache off), so it tests the real website path.
- **Sealed run**: `run_final_v8.sh` gains a `long` step: `sealed_v8_long` for v7 and v8, plus a v8-without-
  LONG_SUMMARY feature arm. The stamp now also records conv.py, server.py, longsum.py, pref_phrases.py, long_eval.py
  and facts.db's size and date.
  - Validation-matrix rule (8 Nov): ≥ 5/6 with the switch on, and multi-turn unchanged.
  - This step was added before the freeze (decision log).
